Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Shift Pro ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Work-related data you input: shifts, hours worked, earnings, job details, expenses.
  • This data is entered voluntarily by you and stored locally on your device.

1.2 Device Information

We may collect information about your device, such as device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and analytics.

1.3 Usage Data

Information on how you use the App, including features accessed, time spent, and interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality, including tracking work hours and calculating earnings.
  • To improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • To respond to your inquiries or support requests.
  • We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using secure database technology (Room database).
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
  • However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not share your personal data with third parties.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Third-Party Services

  • The App may integrate with third-party services (e.g., for exporting data). Any data shared with such services is subject to their privacy policies.
  • Currently, the App does not use cloud services or external APIs.

6. Your Rights

  • You have the right to access, update, or delete your data stored in the App.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • For any requests regarding your data, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App or on our website.
